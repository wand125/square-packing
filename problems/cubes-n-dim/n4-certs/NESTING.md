# Nesting of the canonical certificates

Each line gives the cube removed from the next larger certificate (index in that certificate's canonical order,
0-based), its number of rotated coordinate planes and its number of contacts, by the rule of `SPEC-ALG.md`.

| from k | to k | removed cube | rotated planes | contacts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 29 | 28 | 17 | 3 | 12 |
| 28 | 27 | 16 | 3 | 12 |
| 27 | 26 | 13 | 2 | 22 |
| 25 | 24 | 12 | 2 | 24 |
| 24 | 23 | 21 | 1 | 10 |
| 23 | 22 | 13 | 1 | 9 |
| 22 | 21 | 15 | 1 | 9 |
| 21 | 20 | 12 | 1 | 8 |
| 20 | 19 | 11 | 1 | 8 |
| 19 | 18 | 7 | 1 | 7 |

The k = 25 packing is the product of two 5-square packings (16 axis-parallel cubes, 8 turned in one plane, 1 turned in two); the k = 29 packing is the opened 25-cube frame with cubes in cross cells, side 2 + 2√2/3.
