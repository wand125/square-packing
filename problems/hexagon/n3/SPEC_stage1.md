# Stage 1 claim for three unit squares in the regular hexagon

This is the specification of what `stage1_cert.json` should establish. It is written for an
independent re-implementation. It deliberately does not describe how the first checker works.

## Container

- H is the closed regular hexagon with side L = (1 + sqrt3)/2.
- Coordinates follow the certificate's frame: centre G = (L, L*sqrt3/2) and flat bottom. The
  vertices are (L/2, 0), (3L/2, 0), (2L, L*sqrt3/2), (3L/2, L*sqrt3), (L/2, L*sqrt3) and
  (0, L*sqrt3/2).
- All coordinates in the certificate lie in Q(sqrt3). The string form is "a" or "a+b*sqrt3" or
  "a-b*sqrt3", with a and b rationals such as "3/4".

## Poses

- Q(c, theta) = c + R_theta [-1/2, 1/2]^2 is the closed unit square with centre c and angle theta.
- A pose is admissible if Q(c, theta) lies inside H, so that all four corners are in the closed
  hexagon.
- Angles are parametrised by u = tan(theta/2).

## Symmetry reduction (D6)

- The weighted point set is invariant under D6 about G, generated by the rotation by pi/3 about G
  and the reflection x -> 2L - x. Check this exactly; the certificate has "symmetry": "D6".
- The square has period pi/2 in theta. The rotation by pi/3 sends theta to theta + pi/3, which is
  theta - pi/6 modulo pi/2, and the reflection sends theta to -theta. So every pose is D6-equivalent
  to a pose with theta in [0, pi/12].
- Use the representatives with u in [0, U], U = 1/7. Note that 2*arctan(1/7) > pi/12, so this
  covers [0, pi/12].

## Excused boxes

- The field "excused" lists boxes [x0, x1, y0, y1, u0, u1] with rational endpoints. They are boxes
  in (centre x, centre y, u), in the certificate frame, with u in [0, 1/7].
- A reduced pose (c, u) is excused if it lies in at least one of these boxes (closed boxes).
- A sound checker may excuse a region of poses only if that region lies inside a single excused
  box. That is what the first checker does. Covering by a union of boxes is also sound, but it is
  not needed.
- The set N of excused poses is, by definition, the union of the D6 images of these boxes. Later
  stages use exactly this N.

## The claim

For every admissible pose whose reduced representative (u in [0, 1/7]) is NOT excused:

    sum of w_p over the points p in Q(c, theta)  >=  1

Here "in" means closed containment, so a point on the boundary of the square counts.

Budget: the sum of all weights is 749319/250000 < 3. Check this exactly.

## Consequence (not part of the check)

- Suppose three unit squares with disjoint interiors fit in a hexagon of side L' < L.
- Scale the centres from G by the factor L/L'. This gives three pairwise disjoint closed unit
  squares in H.
- If all three were outside N, they would capture a total weight of at least 3, but the total is
  less than 3.
- So at least one of the three has a pose in N.
